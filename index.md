# Dualtop

**Your PC and Mac in your pocket.** Dualtop turns your iPhone, and the foldable iPhone Duo above all, into a little laptop: a real keyboard and trackpad on one half and a screen on the other, with your computers a tap away. And a workspace of its own for when you're not connected.

- **Any Windows PC:** [Dualtop for Windows](https://github.com/SamuelAirs/dualtop-windows/releases) gives smooth video, the PC's sound and game controllers, on Windows Home as well as Pro.
- **Windows Pro:** Remote Desktop, built in.
- **Macs, Linux and the Raspberry Pi:** VNC and Screen Sharing.
